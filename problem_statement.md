Lifestyle Track: Beating the Burnout
Stress & Workload Manager
Background:
Uni students are juggling a lot at once with assignments, part-time jobs, social commitments, errands, plus just keeping up physically and mentally. It's rarely one big thing that causes burnout. It's everything piling up together without anyone noticing until it's too late.
The problem:
Most students don't really know how much they're carrying at any given point, or which part of their life is actually overloaded. So they keep saying yes to things, put off the stuff that feels less urgent, and end up running on empty before they realise what happened.
What the solution should solve:
Build an app that gives students a clear picture of their load across different areas (mental, time, physical, social, errands) and actually helps them do something about it before burnout hits. It shouldn't just track and report. Instead, it should help students rebalance what they're carrying and push them toward recovery, like rest or getting out of the house. Make sure it's usable, accessible, and something students would actually keep open on their phone.
Examples of features:
For example, think of a workload visualiser showing load by category, with something like "You're at 90% capacity this week" as a quick sanity-check. A stress tracker to log how you're feeling over time. A load balancer that groups tasks together and pushes back lower-priority ones when things get hectic. And some kind of recovery nudge, suggesting sleep, downtime, or a hangout when someone's clearly overloaded. Teams are free to build any features with any tech that fits.
